FAQ

Asked at the host stand
every night.

Do I grill the food myself?
Mostly, yes — that's the point. Staff grill certain cuts tableside when they need a practiced hand, and first-timers get a quick coaching session with the first round.
Is there a time limit on tables?
Standard tables are booked in 2-hour slots; group tables and the private room in 2.5-hour slots. If the room allows, we extend — just ask your server.
Do you take walk-ins?
We keep a few bar seats and one grill table for walk-ins, but Friday and Saturday nights fill early. A reservation is the safer bet.
Are there vegetarian options?
Yes — a mushroom grilling platter, vegetable banchan, tofu and mushroom stews, and stone-bowl bibimbap. Tell your server and the kitchen will guide the order.
Can you handle allergies?
Describe any allergy when ordering; the kitchen confirms what it can and cannot accommodate. Marinated cuts contain soy and wheat; banchan varies daily.
