The philosophy

The grill is the stove. The table is the kitchen.

In a Korean BBQ room, cooking isn't hidden behind a wall — it's the social event. The rhythm of the meal belongs to the table: when to order the next cut, when to wrap, when to pause for banchan.

Our job is to keep the fire steady and the side dishes coming. Yours is to eat at your own pace.

"A good table doesn't need a script. It needs good meat, live coals and one more leaf."
Cuts & marinades

Two camps, one grill.

Unmarinated cuts — brisket, ribeye, belly — get only salt and good heat; the meat does the talking. Marinated cuts carry our kitchen's kinship flavors: soy, garlic, pear, gochujang. On the grill they caramelize fast and disappear faster.

Each cut sheet on the menu lists its cooking time and pairing so you can build a progression for your table.
